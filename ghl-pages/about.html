Our Story

From the Field to the Software

TradeFlow started in 2019 when our founder, a third-generation contractor, got frustrated watching leads slip through the cracks. Between managing crews, handling estimates, and trying to keep customers happy, there was never enough time—and the existing software was either too complicated or didn't understand how trade businesses actually work.

So we built something better. We talked to hundreds of roofers, plumbers, HVAC techs, and general contractors. We learned what they actually needed—not what software companies thought they needed.

The result is TradeFlow: a CRM that speaks your language, fits your workflow, and actually helps you grow. No fancy features you'll never use. No steep learning curve. Just powerful tools that work the way you do.

Today, we're proud to serve over 1,200 trade businesses across the country. But we're just getting started. Our team is committed to building the best software for trades—period.
